## 1. Executive Summary & Historical Background

For over seven decades, the rapid urbanization of the National Capital Territory (NCT) of Delhi outpaced the formal public housing delivery systems envisioned in the Delhi Development Act, 1957. As a result, millions of residents settled in unplanned developments constructed on agricultural land, Gram Sabha land, and government-acquired land.

These settlements evolved outside formal municipal planning frameworks, giving rise to an estimated **1,800 unauthorized colonies (UCs)** housing approximately 40 to 50 lakh (4–5 million) people—nearly one-fourth of Delhi's total population.

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                      DELHI UNPLANNED SETTLEMENTS                        │
│                           (~1,800 Colonies)                             │
└────────────────────────────────────┬────────────────────────────────────┘
                                     │
                 ┌───────────────────┴───────────────────┐
                 ▼                                       ▼
    ┌───────────────────────────┐           ┌───────────────────────────┐
    │  1,731 REGULAR COLONIES   │           │   69 AFFLUENT COLONIES    │
    │ (Low & Mid-Income UCs)    │           │ (Luxury Enclaves / Farms) │
    ├───────────────────────────┤           ├───────────────────────────┤
    │ • High density, small plots│           │ • Low density, large plots│
    │ • Eligible for PM-UDAY    │           │ • Excluded from PM-UDAY   │
    │ • Subsidized token fees   │           │ • Separate policy planned │
    │ • Conveyance / Auth Slips │           │ • Protected under Morator.│
    └───────────────────────────┘           └───────────────────────────┘
```

Historically, residents held properties via informal instruments:
- General Power of Attorney (**GPA**)
- Agreement to Sell (**ATS**)
- Will / Testamentary disposition
- Possession Letter and Cash/Cheque Receipts

Following the landmark Supreme Court of India ruling in *Suraj Lamp & Industries Pvt. Ltd. vs. State of Haryana (2011)* (183 DLT 1 SC), transactions through GPA/ATS were declared legally incapable of transferring de jure immovable property title. Consequently, millions of property holders were locked in legal uncertainty, unable to secure institutional home loans, register deeds at the Sub-Registrar of Assurances, or obtain sanctioned building plans from local urban bodies.

In 2019, the Union Government enacted a landmark legislative intervention—the **National Capital Territory of Delhi (Recognition of Property Rights of Residents in Unauthorised Colonies) Act, 2019** (Act No. 45 of 2019)—operationalized through the **PM-UDAY Scheme** (*Pradhan Mantri - Unauthorized Colonies in Delhi Awas Adhikar Yojana*). PM-UDAY confers formal ownership titles on residents of **1,731 regular unauthorized colonies**, while intentionally segregating **69 affluent unauthorized colonies** for separate policy formulation.

## 2. Primary Statutory & Legislative Architecture

The governance of unauthorized settlements in Delhi rests upon two pillars: **Interim Moratorium Protection** (preventing demolition) and **Property Rights Regularization** (conferring ownership).

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                          STATUTORY ARCHITECTURE                             │
├──────────────────────────────────────┬──────────────────────────────────────┤
│    PROTECTIVE MORATORIUM (SHIELD)    │   PROPERTY RIGHTS RECOGNITION (DEED) │
├──────────────────────────────────────┼──────────────────────────────────────┤
│ NCT of Delhi Laws (Special           │ NCT of Delhi (Recognition of Property │
│ Provisions) Second Act, 2011         │ Rights of Residents in UCs) Act, 2019│
│ (Act No. 20 of 2011; Ext. to 2026)   │ (Act No. 45 of 2019)                 │
├──────────────────────────────────────┼──────────────────────────────────────┤
│ • Moratorium against demolition      │ • Direct conferment of freehold /    │
│ • Covers ALL pre-2014 colonies       │   ownership title via DDA            │
│ • Applies to 1,731 + 69 Colonies     │ • Dual Title: Conveyance Deed or     │
│ • Protects physical possession       │   Authorisation Slip                 │
│ • Negative covenant on civic bodies  │ • Applies EXCLUSIVELY to 1,731 UCs   │
└──────────────────────────────────────┴──────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### 2.1 NCT of Delhi (Recognition of Property Rights) Act, 2019 (Act 45 of 2019)

- **Passed by Parliament:** Lok Sabha (Nov 28, 2019), Rajya Sabha (Dec 4, 2019).
- **Presidential Assent:** December 11, 2019; published in the Gazette of India Extraordinary, Part II, Section 1 (Dec 11, 2019).
- **Statutory Objective:** To recognize property rights of ownership, transfer, and mortgage for residents in unauthorized colonies on an "as-is, where-is" basis, regularizing past GPA/ATS transactions upon payment of nominal developmental charges.

#### Key Statutory Provisions:

1. **Section 2(1)(a) – Definition of Unauthorized Colony:**  
   *"Unauthorised colony means a colony or development situated on private land or agricultural land or Government land which has been delineated by the Delhi Development Authority in the manner as may be prescribed."*

2. **Section 2(1)(b) – Resident:**  
   Any person having physical possession of property on the basis of a registered or unregistered GPA, Agreement to Sell, Will, or possession letter executed prior to the cutoff date.

3. **Section 3 – Recognition and Conferment of Property Rights:**  
   Authorizes the Central Government and DDA to execute Conveyance Deeds (for Government / DDA / Gram Sabha land) or Authorisation Slips (for Private land) in favor of eligible residents upon payment of prescribed regularisation charges.

4. **Section 4 – Validation of Past Transactions:**  
   Statutorily overrides Stamp Act and Registration Act restrictions for the specific purpose of regularizing eligible pre-2015 informal transfers under the PM-UDAY framework.

### 2.2 PM-UDAY Regulations, 2019 (G.S.R. 814(E) & S.O. Notifications)

Notified by the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs (MoHUA) vide **Notification No. G.S.R. 814(E)** on **October 29, 2019**, these regulations detail the operational machinery of PM-UDAY:

- **Regulation 3(1) – Scope and Exclusions:** Excludes 69 specific unauthorized colonies inhabited by the affluent sections of society, as well as properties falling in notified ecological, archaeological, or infrastructure corridors.
- **Regulation 4 – Cutoff Dates:** Fixes existence cutoff to **June 1, 2014** and built-up density cutoff to **January 1, 2015**.
- **Regulation 5 – Calculation of Charges:** Categorizes land into Category 1 (Private Land: 0.5% of adjoining circle rate) and Category 2 (Government Land: 1%–2.5% of circle rate based on plot size).
- **Regulation 6 & 7 – Procedure & Title Execution:** Mandates online GIS demarcation, keying of property boundaries, field verification, and issuance of Conveyance Deeds or Authorisation Slips.
- **Statutory Orders (S.O.):**
  - **S.O. 3912(E)** (Oct 29, 2019): Delineation guidelines and SOP for boundary demarcation.
  - **S.O. 4086(E)** (Nov 12, 2019): Vesting DDA with powers of execution and title generation.
  - **S.O. 4599(E)** (Dec 24, 2019): Notification of the Schedule of 69 Affluent Colonies.

### 2.3 NCT of Delhi Laws (Special Provisions) Second Act, 2011 (Act 20 of 2011)

Originally enacted in December 2011 to replace temporary ordinances dating back to 2006, this statute creates a statutory bar against municipal demolition, sealing, or eviction:

- **Legislative History:** Successively extended by Parliament through amendment acts in 2014, 2017, 2020, and December 2023.
- **Current Legal Status:** Valid and enforceable **through December 31, 2026**.
- **Section 3 – Moratorium Provisions:** Prohibits local authorities (MCD, DDA, NDMC, GNCTD) from taking punitive action against unauthorized colonies, village abadi extensions, and slum clusters built prior to June 1, 2014.

### 2.4 Interplay Between Moratorium Protection and Title Recognition

| Feature | Special Provisions Act, 2011 | PM-UDAY Act, 2019 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Legal Nature** | Negative Protection (Moratorium / Immunity) | Positive Title Conferment (Ownership Rights) |
| **Applicability** | Both Regular (1,731) and Affluent (69) Colonies | Strictly 1,731 Regular Colonies Only |
| **Legal Impact** | Prevents sealing and demolition | Grants freehold title / transfer / mortgage rights |
| **Financial Cost** | Nil (Statutory Protection) | Prescribed regularisation and conversion charges |
| **Validity / Tenure** | Temporary (Currently extended to Dec 31, 2026) | Permanent (Indefeasible property title) |

## 3. Delhi Colony Classification Taxonomy

Delhi's urban matrix comprises several distinct residential typologies, each governed by different administrative and legal codes.

### 3.1 Taxonomy Matrix

| Typology Category | Colony Count | Governing Statute / Authority | Title Recognition Status | Demarcation Status |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Regular Unauthorized Colonies** | **1,731** | PM-UDAY Act, 2019 & Regs 2019 (DDA) | **Conveyance Deed** / **Authorisation Slip** | Demarcated via 2015 Cartosat GIS Boundary Maps |
| **2. Affluent Unauthorized Colonies** | **69** | NCT Special Provisions Act, 2011 (MoHUA/DDA) | **Excluded from PM-UDAY** (Separate policy TBD) | Identified in Gazette Schedule (S.O. 4599(E)) |
| **3. Urbanized Village Abadi (Lal Dora)** | ~569 Villages | DMC Act 1957 / Delhi Land Reforms Act (GNCTD) | Freehold / PM-SVAMITVA Property Cards | Cadastral Revenue Village Khasra Maps |
| **4. Extended Lal Dora (ELD) Clusters** | Included in UC list | Revenue Dept GNCTD / DDA PM-UDAY | Covered under PM-UDAY if listed in 1,731 UCs | Demarcated under ELD Registration Series |
| **5. Planned Residential Colonies** | Notified zones | Master Plan for Delhi (MPD-2021/2041) / DDA | Complete de jure freehold / leasehold titles | Fully surveyed municipal layouts |
| **6. Resettlement Colonies** | 45 Colonies | DDA / MCD / Land & Building Department | Leasehold to Freehold conversion schemes | Municipal layout surveys |
| **7. JJ Bastis (Slum Clusters)** | ~675 Clusters | Delhi Urban Shelter Improvement Board (DUSIB) | In-situ Slum Rehabilitation (PMAY Urban) | DUSIB GIS Spatial Registry |

### 3.2 Detailed Typology Profiles

#### A. Regular Unauthorized Colonies (1,731)
Characterized by high built-up density, mixed plot sizes (typically 25 sq. meters to 250 sq. meters), narrow internal streets (often under 6 meters), and predominantly working-class, lower-middle, and middle-class populations. Established primarily by unapproved private colonizers subdividing agricultural parcels without formal zonal layout approvals. Eligible for full PM-UDAY regularization.

#### B. Affluent Unauthorized Colonies (69)
Characterized by low-to-medium built-up density, expansive plot sizes (ranging from 500 sq. yards to multiple acres), luxury farmhouses, and high-value residential bungalows. Examples include Sainik Farm, Anupam Garden, and Freedom Fighter Enclave. Statutorily excluded from PM-UDAY nominal fee regularization under Regulation 3(1).

#### C. Urbanized Village Abadi (Lal Dora) & Extended Lal Dora (ELD)
- **Lal Dora:** Historically designated village habitation zones exempted from building bye-laws under Section 507 of the Delhi Municipal Corporation Act, 1957.
- **Extended Lal Dora (ELD):** Agricultural lands immediately abutting the Lal Dora where villagers constructed dwellings as families expanded. When unregulated development occurred in ELD areas, colonies registered under the `ELD` series (e.g., `12-(ELD)`, `74-(ELD)`) and are eligible under PM-UDAY.

#### D. Slum Clusters / Jhuggi Jhopri (JJ) Bastis (~675)
Informal shanty settlements on encroached public lands (railways, DDA, MCD). Governed by the Delhi Urban Shelter Improvement Board (DUSIB) Act, 2010. These settlements are not covered by PM-UDAY and are addressed under the *Jahan Jhuggi Wahan Makaan* in-situ rehabilitation policy.

### 4.1 Statutory Exclusion Mechanism (Regulation 3(1))

Under Sub-Regulation (1) of Regulation 3 of the *NCT of Delhi (Recognition of Property Rights of Residents in Unauthorised Colonies) Regulations, 2019*:

> *"These regulations shall apply to all unauthorized colonies in the National Capital Territory of Delhi, **save and except the sixty-nine unauthorized colonies inhabited by affluent sections of the society** specified in the Schedule appended to these regulations."*

This exclusion was formally promulgated through **Notification No. S.O. 4599(E)** dated December 24, 2019.

### 4.2 Socio-Economic Rationale & Policy Divergence

The bifurcation between regular and affluent unauthorized colonies was grounded in principles of administrative fairness, socio-economic equity, and revenue integrity:

1. **Subsidized Social Welfare vs. Windfall Gains:**  
   The PM-UDAY scheme was designed with heavily subsidized regularization charges (0.5% to 2.5% of circle rates) to provide housing security to low- and middle-income families. Applying these token rates to multi-crore luxury estates and farmhouses would have resulted in unwarranted private windfall gains at severe cost to public revenue.

2. **Spatial and Infrastructure Dynamics:**  
   Standard unauthorized colonies require significant government capital outlays for basic water, sewerage, road paving, and electrification. Affluent colonies often possess private infrastructure (borewells, private security, captive power backup) but lack formal layout sanctions, master plan zoning compliance, and adequate public road widths.

3. **Separate Policy Deliberations:**  
   MoHUA and DDA constituted high-level expert committees to formulate a distinct policy for the 69 Affluent Colonies. The proposed framework envisions:
   - Higher development and conversion charges pegged to commercial and high-value residential circle rates.
   - Specific Floor Area Ratio (FAR) and ground coverage caps tailored to farmhouse belts.
   - Mandatory surrender of land for road widening and environmental offsets.

### 4.3 Judicial Precedents & High Court Interventions

The status of affluent unauthorized colonies—most notably Sainik Farm—has been the subject of extensive litigation before the Hon'ble High Court of Delhi:

1. **Paardarshita Public Welfare Foundation vs. GNCTD & Ors. (W.P.(C) 4582/2003):**  
   The Delhi High Court monitored illegal construction in Sainik Farm and directed civic agencies to halt all unauthorized building activities while restraining the government from granting blanket amnesty without adequate environmental and developmental charges.

2. **Sainik Farm RWA vs. Union of India (W.P.(C) 6934/2014):**  
   The Resident Welfare Association sought regularisation on par with other unauthorized colonies. The Union Government placed on record that Sainik Farm and 68 other affluent settlements were categorized separately due to their elite character and would be dealt with under a bespoke policy framework.

## 5. PM-UDAY Title Regularization Framework

The PM-UDAY scheme represents a structural shift from precarious informal occupancy to legally enforceable property ownership.

### 5.1 The Dual Title Regime (Conveyance Deed vs Authorisation Slip)

The framework bifurcates title instruments based on underlying land revenue status:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                          PM-UDAY DUAL TITLE REGIME                          │
├──────────────────────────────────────┬──────────────────────────────────────┤
│       CONVEYANCE DEED                │        AUTHORISATION SLIP            │
├──────────────────────────────────────┼──────────────────────────────────────┤
│ • Applicable on:                     │ • Applicable on:                     │
│   - Government Land                  │   - Private Agricultural Land        │
│   - DDA Land                         │   - Unacquired Private Parcels       │
│   - Gram Sabha Land                  │                                      │
│ • Title Effect:                      │ • Title Effect:                      │
│   Direct transfer of freehold        │   Regularizes GPA chain; recognizes  │
│   ownership from the President of    │   property rights to sell, mortgage, │
│   India to the resident.             │   and build without State land deed. │
│ • SRO Registration:                  │ • SRO Registration:                  │
│   Conveyance Deed registered at SRO. │   Authorisation Slip + Transfer Deed │
│                                      │   registered at SRO.                 │
└──────────────────────────────────────┴──────────────────────────────────────┘
```

#### 1. Conveyance Deed
- **Applicable Land:** Government Land, DDA Acquired Land, or Gram Sabha Land vested in the State under Section 81/154 of the Delhi Land Reforms Act, 1954.
- **Legal Effect:** Conveys absolute freehold ownership title from the President of India (acting through the Vice-Chairman, DDA) to the beneficiary. It extinguishes government title and creates a fresh, marketable root of title.

#### 2. Authorisation Slip
- **Applicable Land:** Private Land or Unacquired Agricultural Land where the underlying revenue ownership remained with private individuals, but physical possession was subdivided and transferred via informal GPA/ATS chains.
- **Legal Effect:** Does not convey government land (since the government does not own the underlying parcel). Instead, it confers statutory recognition of property rights—validating the possessory chain, granting building plan eligibility, and authorizing formal registered sale deeds at the Sub-Registrar Office.

### 5.2 Statutory Eligibility Criteria & Cutoff Dates

To qualify for regularisation under PM-UDAY, a property must satisfy four mandatory conditions:

1. **Existence Cutoff Date (June 1, 2014):**  
   The unauthorized colony must have been established and in physical existence prior to **June 1, 2014**.
2. **Construction & Built-up Density Threshold (January 1, 2015):**  
   The specific plot or built-up structure must have been physically occupied/constructed prior to **January 1, 2015**. Vacant plots in colonies that were built up to at least 50% density by January 1, 2015 are also eligible. Satellite imagery captured by Survey of India in 2015 serves as conclusive evidence.
3. **Continuous Title Chain:**  
   The applicant must demonstrate uninterrupted possession through documentary proof executed prior to January 1, 2015.
4. **Non-Prohibited Zone Compliance:**  
   The property must NOT fall within Yamuna Zone 'O', Delhi Ridge, ASI protected monument buffers (100m/200m), or Master Plan Right of Way (ROW).

### 5.3 Mandatory Document Chain & Evidentiary Standards

Applicants must upload a complete set of documentary evidence onto the PM-UDAY portal:

```
[Initial Allotment / Farmer Sale] ──► [GPA / ATS / Will / Receipt] ──► [Electricity Bill] ──► [GIS Keying]
```

1. **Primary Title Documents (Executed on or before 01.01.2015):**
   - **General Power of Attorney (GPA)** executed by the previous owner.
   - **Agreement to Sell (ATS)** establishing agreed consideration.
   - **Will** / Testamentary instrument.
   - **Receipt of Payment** (cash receipt, bank counterfoil, or cheque acknowledgment).
   - **Possession Letter** showing physical handover.
2. **Proof of Physical Occupancy:**
   - Electricity connection bill (meter installation date or active billing prior to 01.01.2015).
   - Water connection bill from Delhi Jal Board (DJB).
   - Property tax assessment or payment receipt (MCD).
   - Aadhaar Card / Voter ID Card matching the property address.
3. **Geo-Spatial Identity:**
   - Empanelled GIS Agency Survey Certificate and Geo-coordinate polygon key.
   - Ground-level site photographs showing front elevation and boundary corners.

### 5.4 Financial Structure: Computation of Regularization Charges

Under Regulation 5 of the PM-UDAY Regulations 2019, regularization charges are computed based on plot area, built-up area, land category, and the circle rate of the nearest notified planned residential colony:

| Parameter | Category 1: Private Land | Category 2: Government / DDA / Gram Sabha Land |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Plot Size ≤ 100 sq. meters** | 0.5% of Circle Rate of adjoining colony | 1.0% of Circle Rate of adjoining colony |
| **Plot Size 101 – 250 sq. meters** | 0.5% of Circle Rate of adjoining colony | 1.5% of Circle Rate of adjoining colony |
| **Plot Size > 250 sq. meters** | 0.5% of Circle Rate of adjoining colony | 2.5% of Circle Rate of adjoining colony |
| **Vacant Land Component** | Standard rate on land area | Standard rate + 0.5% surcharge on vacant land area |
| **Annual Escalation** | 8% compounding annual escalation applicable from **April 1, 2022** onwards | 8% compounding annual escalation applicable from **April 1, 2022** onwards |

*Adjoining Circle Rate Reference:* DDA maps each unauthorized colony to the nearest notified municipal residential circle (Categorized A to H under GNCTD Circle Rate Rules).

### 6.1 Cartographic Base & Satellite Demarcation

- **Remote Sensing Platform:** High-resolution optical imagery from the **Cartosat-2** series and aerial ortho-photos captured in **2015**.
- **Collaborative Execution:** DDA Geospatial Cell in institutional collaboration with the **Survey of India (SOI)** and the Department of Revenue, GNCTD.
- **Cadastral Overlay:** Vector boundary polygons of unauthorized colonies are superimposed over georeferenced revenue village Khasra maps at 1:2500 scale to determine exact land parcel ownership.

### 6.2 Two-Stage Publication Protocol (Base vs Finalised Boundaries)

1. **Stage 1: 2015 Base Boundary Satellite PDF:**  
   The initial draft map delineating the external perimeter of the colony as observed in 2015 satellite imagery. Published on the DDA portal for public scrutiny and RWA feedback.
2. **Stage 2: Finalised Boundary PDF:**  
   The statutory, finalized demarcation map approved after resolving ground-truthing disputes, cadastral boundary alignments, and municipal road Right of Way demarcations.

### 6.3 Clustering Architecture & Registration Cross-Referencing

In high-density urban clusters (e.g., Sangam Vihar, Burari, Sant Nagar, Kirari, Uttam Nagar), multiple registered unauthorized colonies share continuous, contiguous physical perimeters. DDA grouped these into unified **Boundary Map Numbers**:
- A single Map Number (e.g., `Map No. 452`) may encompass multiple Registration Numbers (e.g., `Reg. No. 873`, `Reg. No. 874`, `Reg. No. 875`).
- The DDA database notes these relationships under `Remarks / Clustering, if any` (e.g., `"(1) REGD. NO.873_Sangam Vihar Block C, Part-II Delhi-62"`).

### 6.4 Special Boundary Scenarios (Omissions & SOP Restrictions)

Analysis of the official DDA delineated boundary registry reveals two critical administrative scenarios:

1. **Omitted Map Numbers (15 Entries):**  
   Map numbers `[292, 1332, 1349, 1378, 1389, 1398, 1399, 1411, 1412, 1453, 1455, 1462, 1487, 1494, 1524]` are absent from the DDA HTML boundary table. These represent colonies that were merged into contiguous clusters or withheld during portal migration.
2. **SOP Exclusions (152 Entries):**  
   152 rows in the boundary registry bear the official remark: `BOUNDARY OF COLONY CAN NOT BE DELINEATED AS PER "SOP"`. In these cases, the shapefile could not be finalized due to severe Khasra overlaps, pending court litigation, or extensive intersection with prohibited forest/riverbed areas.

## 7. Prohibited & Statutory Exclusion Zones

Properties falling inside statutory exclusion zones are strictly ineligible for PM-UDAY title regularisation, regardless of colony boundary overlap.

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                        STATUTORY EXCLUSION ZONES                            │
├───────────────────┬─────────────────────────┬───────────────────────────────┤
│ Exclusion Zone    │ Statutory Basis         │ Restrictions / Buffer Width   │
├───────────────────┼─────────────────────────┼───────────────────────────────┤
│ Yamuna Zone 'O'   │ MPD-2021 / NGT Orders   │ Entire active floodplain      │
│ Delhi Ridge       │ SC Orders / Forest Act  │ Reserved & Notified Forests   │
│ ASI Monuments     │ AMASR Act, 1958         │ 100m Prohibited / 200m Reg.   │
│ Master Plan ROW   │ MPD-2021 Transportation │ 30m, 45m, 60m Master Plan ROW │
│ Public Utilities  │ Master Plan Zoning      │ Sites for Schools/Hospitals   │
└───────────────────┴─────────────────────────┴───────────────────────────────┘
```

### 7.1 Yamuna Riverbed & Active Floodplains (Zone 'O')
- **Statutory Authority:** Master Plan for Delhi (Zone 'O' River Conservation Zone) and National Green Tribunal (NGT) directives in *Manoj Misra vs. Union of India* (OA No. 6/2012).
- **Mandate:** Absolute prohibition on any regularisation, construction, or title conferment within the active riverbed and 1-in-25-year flood inundation zones.

### 7.2 Delhi Ridge & Reserved Forest Lands
- **Statutory Authority:** Indian Forest Act, 1927; Delhi Preservation of Trees Act, 1994; Supreme Court Ridge Management Board orders in *M.C. Mehta vs. Union of India* (W.P.(C) 4677/1985).
- **Scope:** Central Ridge, Northern Ridge, South-Central Ridge (Mehrauli-Vasant Kunj), Southern Ridge (Asola-Bhatti Wildlife Sanctuary). Zero regularization permitted on notified forest land.

### 7.3 Ancient Monuments & Archaeological Sites (ASI Buffers)
- **Statutory Authority:** Ancient Monuments and Archaeological Sites and Remains Act, 1958 (AMASR Act, amended 2010).
- **Buffer Regimes:**
  - **100-Meter Prohibited Area:** Zero construction, modification, or title regularisation allowed within 100 meters of Centrally Protected Monuments.
  - **200-Meter Regulated Area:** Construction/repair permitted only with prior statutory No-Objection Certificate (NOC) from the National Monuments Authority (NMA).

### 7.4 Master Plan Right of Way (ROW) & Infrastructure Corridors
- **Statutory Authority:** Delhi Development Act, 1957; MPD-2021/2041 Transportation Networks.
- **Scope:** Properties encroaching on designated Master Plan road Right of Way (ROWs of 30m, 45m, 60m, 100m), metro rail viaduct/tunnel easements, high-tension (HT) power transmission line corridors (CEA safety buffers), and GAIL/IOCL underground gas pipelines are barred from regularisation.

### 7.5 Public Utility Lands & Reserved Social Infrastructure
- Land parcels designated in zonal plans for community facilities (government schools, hospitals, dispensaries, sewage treatment plants, parks, electric sub-stations) are excluded from private title conferment.

## 8. Comprehensive Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

#### Q1: What is the primary objective of the PM-UDAY Scheme?
**A:** The PM-UDAY (*Pradhan Mantri - Unauthorized Colonies in Delhi Awas Adhikar Yojana*) scheme was enacted through the *NCT of Delhi (Recognition of Property Rights of Residents in Unauthorised Colonies) Act, 2019* (Act 45 of 2019) to confer formal, legally recognized property ownership, mortgage, and transfer rights upon residents of 1,731 regular unauthorized colonies in Delhi. It replaces informal GPA/ATS chains with registered Conveyance Deeds and Authorisation Slips.

#### Q2: What is the legal difference between a Conveyance Deed and an Authorisation Slip?
**A:** 
- A **Conveyance Deed** is issued when the property sits on **Government Land, DDA Acquired Land, or Gram Sabha Land**. It directly transfers freehold ownership title from the Government of India to the resident.
- An **Authorisation Slip** is issued when the property sits on **Private Agricultural / Unacquired Land**. It validates the resident's title and GPA chain, conferring the legal right to execute registered transfer deeds, obtain bank mortgages, and apply for building sanctions without transferring State land.

#### Q3: Why are the 69 Affluent Colonies (e.g., Sainik Farm, Anupam Garden) excluded from PM-UDAY?
**A:** Under Regulation 3(1) of the PM-UDAY Regulations 2019, the 69 Affluent Colonies are excluded because they comprise high-value luxury farmhouses, large estates (500–5000+ sq. meters), and palatial bungalows. PM-UDAY was conceived as a welfare measure with nominal, subsidized regularization fees for low- and middle-income families on small plots. Applying token charges to multi-crore luxury estates would result in significant public revenue loss and policy inequity. A separate policy framework with higher development and conversion charges is under deliberation.

#### Q4: Are the 69 Affluent Colonies protected from demolition or sealing?
**A:** Yes. Under the *National Capital Territory of Delhi Laws (Special Provisions) Second Act, 2011* (as amended through December 2023), all unauthorized settlements established prior to June 1, 2014—including the 69 Affluent Colonies—enjoy statutory protection against demolition, sealing, or punitive eviction through **December 31, 2026**.

#### Q5: What are the strict cutoff dates for PM-UDAY eligibility?
**A:** 
1. **Colony Existence:** The unauthorized colony must have existed prior to **June 1, 2014**.
2. **Construction Status:** The property must have had physical construction/built-up status prior to **January 1, 2015** (verified via 2015 Cartosat satellite imagery). Vacant plots in colonies that had achieved 50% built-up density by Jan 1, 2015 are also eligible.
3. **Document Chain:** Primary transfer documents (GPA, ATS, Will, Receipt) must be dated on or before **January 1, 2015**.

#### Q6: Can a property located within an unauthorized colony be rejected if it falls in a prohibited zone?
**A:** Yes. Individual property applications within a delineated unauthorized colony will be rejected if the property falls in:
- **Zone 'O'** (Yamuna riverbed and active floodplains).
- **Delhi Ridge** or reserved forest areas.
- **ASI 100-meter Prohibited Zone** around centrally protected monuments.
- **Master Plan Road Right of Way (ROW)** or public infrastructure utility corridors.

#### Q7: How is the boundary of an unauthorized colony delineated?
**A:** DDA's Geospatial Cell, in collaboration with the **Survey of India (SOI)** and GNCTD Revenue Department, mapped boundary polygons using 2015 high-resolution Cartosat satellite imagery and superimposed them onto georeferenced revenue village Khasra maps. Draft maps (2015 Base Boundary Satellite PDF) were published for public and RWA objections before issuing Finalised Boundary PDFs.

#### Q8: Can a resident obtain a bank home loan after receiving a PM-UDAY title?
**A:** Yes. Once the Conveyance Deed or Authorisation Slip is registered at the Sub-Registrar Office (SRO), it constitutes a clear, marketable, and unencumbered title. Scheduled commercial banks and housing finance corporations recognize registered PM-UDAY deeds for sanctioning home loans, property renovation loans, and mortgages.

#### Q9: Can building plans be sanctioned after regularization under PM-UDAY?
**A:** Yes. Property owners with registered Conveyance Deeds or Authorisation Slips can submit online building sanction applications via the Municipal Corporation of Delhi (MCD) **SWAGAM Portal** under the simplified building bye-laws for unauthorized colonies.

#### Q10: How are PM-UDAY regularisation charges computed?
**A:** Regularization charges are calculated based on:
1. Plot area and built-up area.
2. Land category: Category 1 (Private Land: 0.5% of adjoining circle rate) vs Category 2 (Government Land: 1.0% to 2.5% of circle rate depending on plot size).
3. The circle rate of the adjoining notified planned residential colony.
4. Compounded annual escalation of 8% applied from April 1, 2022 onwards.

#### Q11: What does "BOUNDARY OF COLONY CAN NOT BE DELINEATED AS PER SOP" mean?
**A:** This remark appears for 152 colonies in the DDA boundary registry where boundary shapefiles could not be finalized under the Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) due to complex Khasra boundary disputes, extensive overlap with prohibited forest/riverbed lands, or pending litigation. Residents in these colonies must await field-level dispute resolution by DDA and the Revenue Department.

#### Q12: Why are some colonies grouped under a single Map Number?
**A:** Where multiple registered unauthorized colonies or sub-blocks (e.g., Sangam Vihar Blocks A through L) form a single continuous, contiguous built-up cluster, DDA assigned a single unified boundary Map Number to avoid artificial internal boundaries. Individual colony registration numbers are recorded in the `Remarks / Clustering` field.

## 10. Glossary of Legal, Revenue, and Urban Planning Terms

- **Agreement to Sell (ATS):** A contractual agreement detailing terms of property transfer between buyer and seller; does not confer title without formal registration.
- **Ancient Monument (ASI Buffer):** A heritage structure protected under the AMASR Act 1958. Enforces a 100-meter prohibited buffer (no construction) and a 200-meter regulated buffer (NOC required from National Monuments Authority).
- **Authorisation Slip:** A statutory property instrument issued by DDA for regularized properties on private agricultural land under PM-UDAY, recognizing possession and authorizing registered transfers.
- **Cartosat-2:** High-resolution optical Earth observation satellite series deployed by ISRO, utilized by Survey of India to create the 2015 base cartographic imagery for Delhi colony demarcation.
- **Circle Rate:** The minimum statutory baseline valuation per square meter fixed by GNCTD for property stamp duty assessment in a given administrative zone (Categories A through H).
- **Conveyance Deed:** A formal legal deed executed by DDA transferring absolute freehold ownership rights of government or Gram Sabha land to a private resident.
- **Delineated Boundary:** The official georeferenced spatial polygon demarcating the external perimeter of an unauthorized colony, surveyed by DDA and Survey of India.
- **Extended Lal Dora (ELD):** Agricultural land abutting the historical village Lal Dora settlement where abadi extension took place.
- **Floor Area Ratio (FAR):** The ratio of a building's total floor area relative to the size of the plot of land upon which it is built.
- **General Power of Attorney (GPA):** A legal instrument authorizing an agent to act on behalf of the principal; historically used in Delhi to transfer de facto possession of real estate.
- **Gram Sabha Land:** Common village land vested in the village body under the Delhi Land Reforms Act, 1954, subsequently placed under DDA/GNCTD management upon urbanization.
- **Khasra:** A specific parcel/plot identification number assigned in land revenue cadastral maps.
- **Khatauni:** A land record register showing all land holdings of a person or family within a revenue village.
- **Lal Dora:** The historical red boundary line demarcating village habitation (abadi) land from surrounding agricultural fields, exempt from standard municipal building bye-laws under Section 507 of the DMC Act, 1957.
- **Layout Plan (LOP):** A formal spatial subdivision plan showing plot layouts, internal roads, and open spaces submitted for municipal approval.
- **Right of Way (ROW):** The total reserved strip of public land dedicated for transportation networks, arterial roads, metro corridors, and utilities.
- **Special Provisions Act:** *The NCT of Delhi Laws (Special Provisions) Second Act, 2011*, protecting pre-2014 unauthorized constructions from sealing and demolition through December 31, 2026.
- **SWAGAM:** The simplified single-window online portal operated by MCD for building plan sanctions in regularized unauthorized colonies.
- **Zone 'O':** The Master Plan for Delhi (MPD) ecological planning zone comprising the River Yamuna corridor, riverbed, and active floodplains where all permanent construction is prohibited.
